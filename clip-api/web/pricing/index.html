Pricing FAQ

Questions about billing and credits.

Can I use ClutchClips for free?

Yes. Sign up and get 100 free credits, enough for about 100 minutes of video. No credit card required.

How do credits work?

1 credit processes 1 minute of source video. A 2-hour stream uses 120 credits, and you can make as many clips from it as you want. More clips, searches and reframes of the same upload only cost the minutes they render.

Do unused credits roll over?

Yes. Credits stay in your account while your plan is active.

What if a job fails?

The credits come back to your account automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account menu and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Stripe. Receipts and invoices are in the billing portal.

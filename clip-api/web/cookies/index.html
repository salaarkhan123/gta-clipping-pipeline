The short version: no cookies, no tracking.

Cookies

The ClutchClips website and API set no cookies. We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels, so there is nothing to accept or refuse.

What your browser keeps

The website keeps two things in your browser's local storage. Both are needed for the service to work, and neither is sent to anyone else.

ItemWhat it is forHow long
cc_tokenKeeps you logged in. It is sent only to the ClutchClips API.Valid for 7 days; removed when you log out
Editor draftsClip edits you have not saved yet, so they are not lost if the page closes.Until you save or discard the edit

You can clear them in your browser's settings at any time. You will then be logged out, and unsaved edits are lost.

Other sites

  • Payments and the billing portal are Stripe's own pages. Stripe sets its own cookies there; see Stripe's cookie policy.
  • Fonts are bundled with the website, so no request goes to Google Fonts or any other font service.
  • The API reference at api.clutchclips.io/docs loads its viewer from the jsDelivr CDN, which sees your IP address and browser details like any website you visit.

Changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics that are not strictly needed, we will update this notice first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Our Privacy Policy covers everything else we collect.

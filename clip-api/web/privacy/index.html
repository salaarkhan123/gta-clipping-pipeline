What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and a password. We store only an argon2 hash of the password; nobody can read the password itself.
  • The IP address you signed up from, kept so we can look into abuse of the service.
  • Whether your email is confirmed, your plan, account status and when the account was created.
  • Your API keys, stored only as a hash, with the name you gave each key, its first characters and when it was last used. Revoked keys stay on record.
  • Your webhook signing secret.
  • Which version of each policy you accepted, how (at sign-up or later) and when.
  • Short-lived request counters, keyed by IP address or account, that enforce rate limits. They are kept in memory for up to one hour.

Your videos and what we make from them

  • The videos you upload or import, with the file name, size, length and a fingerprint (hash) of the file. A video can show your face and voice, other people and chat usernames.
  • For a link import: the link, the site it came from, and a log of your account, the link, your confirmation that you own the video or may clip it, and the time.
  • The transcript of what is said in the video (words with times) and a search index of the video.
  • The clips we make: video, thumbnail, captions, preview and exports, plus the titles, hook, description, hashtags, rating and reason the AI writes for each clip.
  • The options you choose for a job, including any free-text prompt and project name, your edits in the editor, and the looks (templates) you save.
  • Which clips you keep, like or bookmark, and the measurements we use to rank clips. We use these to improve how clips are ranked.

Payments

  • Stripe collects your card details on its own pages. They never reach us.
  • We keep your Stripe customer and subscription ids, your subscription status, a record of each payment (Stripe reference, amount, credits granted, refunds) and a ledger of every credit added to or taken from your account.

Email

We send you email to confirm your address (a link valid for 48 hours) and to reset your password (a link valid for one hour). We do not send marketing email.

Logs and errors

  • Our servers log each request: IP address, path and result. Imports log your account id, the site and the first 200 characters of the link. Billing logs record your account id, plan and invoice id.
  • When error monitoring is on, error reports go to Sentry. We do not turn on Sentry's option to send extra personal data.
  • If you set a webhook, we record each delivery: job id, your URL and the response code.

The website

The website sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your browser keeps your login token and unsent editor drafts in its own storage. See the Cookie Notice.

Faces and voices in your videos: we detect where a face is so we can place the facecam in the clip. This finds a box on the screen. It does not create a face template and it does not identify anyone.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use it to work out sensitive things about you, such as health, ethnicity, religion or sexual orientation.

AI processing

  • Transcription runs on our own server (faster-whisper). Your audio does not leave it.
  • Visual search and face detection run on our own server. Nothing leaves it.
  • The AI judge writes each clip's titles, hook, rating and reason and helps choose where the clip starts and ends. It uses Anthropic's Claude API. For each clip, Anthropic receives one still frame from the clip, an excerpt of the clip's timed transcript, the scores that made us pick the moment, and the prompt you typed for the job, if any. Anthropic deletes these inputs and outputs within 30 days (content flagged for a policy breach can be kept up to 2 years) and does not train its models on them.
  • Translation and summaries use Anthropic only when that setting is switched on. Then the transcript text is sent to Anthropic, under the same terms.
  • We do not offer AI-generated thumbnails, and no image-generation company receives your videos or frames.

The AI ranks and titles clips. It makes no decision about you as a person. Its ratings and virality scores are opinions about clips, not forecasts.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal information, in a structured, machine-readable format where you ask for one (access and portability);
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information and your account;
  • stop or limit a use of it, or object to a use based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw a consent you gave (this does not undo what was lawfully done before).

To use a right, email [OWNER: privacy contact email] from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We answer within 30 days (one month under the GDPR). Self-serve export and deletion in the dashboard are not available yet.

Some records we must keep even after a deletion request, such as payment and credit records needed for tax, and the import and takedown records needed to answer legal claims.

You can complain to a privacy regulator at any time:

  • Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada
  • Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta
  • In Quebec: the Commission d'accès à l'information
  • In the EU or UK: the data protection authority where you live or work (in the UK, the ICO)

Security and breaches

We hash passwords with argon2 and store API keys only as hashes. Files are in private storage and are shared only through download links that expire after one hour. All traffic uses HTTPS.

If a breach of security creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and Alberta's Information and Privacy Commissioner (and Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information, or the EU/UK authority, where their laws apply). We keep a record of every breach for at least 24 months.

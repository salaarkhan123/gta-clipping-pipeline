Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start clipping.

How does ClutchClips work?

Upload a stream recording. The AI listens for hype, reads the transcript, finds the strongest moments, cuts them into vertical clips with your facecam on top and captions on the seam, and rates each one.

Can I paste a Twitch or YouTube link?

Not yet. You upload the recording itself, from OBS or the VOD you downloaded from your own channel.

How long does it take?

On our servers a 2-hour VOD takes about 11 minutes to clip once it's uploaded. Upload time depends on your connection.

What does a credit buy?

One credit is one minute of video. A 2-hour stream uses 120 credits, however many clips you make from it. If a job fails, the credits come back automatically.

Is it free to try?

Yes. New accounts get free credits. No card needed.

How long do you keep my files?

Uploads and clips are deleted after 7 days. Download what you want to keep.

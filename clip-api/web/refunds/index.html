What happens to your credits when a job does not finish or a payment is refunded, and how to ask for your money back.

Credits for jobs that do not finish

Starting a job reserves its credits. If the job fails, times out, is cancelled, or stops because our server lost it, every reserved credit goes back to your account automatically. You do not need to ask.

A job that finishes keeps only the credits it used. When a run is charged for the minutes it renders, it gives back whatever it reserved and did not use. Each job is settled exactly once, so credits are never charged or refunded twice.

If content is removed after a copyright takedown, credits held for its unfinished jobs go back too.

When a payment is refunded or disputed

  • If a payment is refunded through Stripe, the credits that payment added are taken back, in proportion to the part refunded. A half refund takes back half of the credits.
  • If you dispute a payment with your bank (a chargeback), all the credits that payment added are taken back.
  • Credits are never taken below a zero balance. Credits you already spent are not owed back.
  • For a yearly plan, a refunded or disputed payment also stops the monthly credits still to come from that year.

Money refunds

  • Credit packs. You can ask for a refund of a credit pack within 14 days of buying it, as long as none of its credits have been used.
  • Used credits. Credits that have been used are not refunded.
  • Subscriptions. You can cancel at any time and keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds of subscription payments are at our discretion, for example when you were charged after cancelling or by mistake.
  • Mistakes and outages. If we charged you in error or the service did not work, tell us and we will put it right.

To ask for a refund, email support@clutchclips.io from the address on your account, with the date and amount of the payment. Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have by law. If you live in the EU or UK, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. Because credits are digital content you can use straight away, you may lose that right once you start using them, if you agreed to that at purchase. Unused purchases stay within the 14-day right.

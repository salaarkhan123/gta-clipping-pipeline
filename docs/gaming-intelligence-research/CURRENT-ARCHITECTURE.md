## Existing evidence and its limits

The RC includes 22 hand-labelled **protected layout boxes** across six source entries, sampled at 200 and 400 seconds for caption-overlap checks. Valorant's seven include score and kill-feed regions. Preserve these as held-out geometry checks. They supply neither per-instance OCR transcripts nor evidence that a clip is worth posting. A private showcase manifest also records one owner-selected hero placement and seven vertical placements, including variants. These are presentation preferences with no matched negatives. [Layout annotations](https://github.com/salaarkhan123/gta-clipping-pipeline/blob/3d684f8e03b02257467e03fb9dc4fcb297499e13/clip-api/scripts/caption_eval_boxes.json)

## Dependencies and existing experimental work

Historical human evidence is narrow but informative. The author of #154 checked the top eight detections from each of two GTA heuristics (death-card colour and wanted-region brightness). Each returned zero true target events among those eight. The same PR reported 10/15 competitor-picked moments recovered with flux and burst features, up from 7/15 on its baseline, after trying multiple weights on the same reaction VOD. That is historical tuning evidence, not an independent test of gaming quality. [U2 experiment and manual checks](https://github.com/salaarkhan123/gta-clipping-pipeline/pull/154)

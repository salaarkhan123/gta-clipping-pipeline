## Decisions

- Parity is taken from Wayin's public docs only. Nothing was observed in their app. Undocumented behaviour is listed in PARITY.md under "Questions for the owner" and is not guessed.
- Our API keeps its own option names with Wayin's values and allowed sets, and today's defaults, until the owner answers PARITY.md questions 1 to 3.
- The orchestrator ships the shared schema (revision 0004) and wires `worker.py`, `main.py`, `config.py` and `models.py`; agents do not write migrations.
- Owner's answers to the seven questions (2026-11-16) are recorded in PARITY.md under "Decisions": our own API
  names with a mapping; Wayin's defaults (render, captions, reframe and hook off, 480p) with the dashboard
  sending explicit choices; `rank_index` and `timeline_index` both returned; Gameplay A/B provisional; enterprise
  switches not built; paid services priced before any integration; our own pricing, from measured costs.
- Merged by the owner: #112 (production-ready clips, `b28bc47`), #113 (contracts and revision 0004, `132b099`),
  #108 (SPEC section 14, `41f79b9`).

- Screenshots `w01` to `w12` arrived (2026-11-16). What they show is recorded in PARITY.md section 6 as observed,
  not verified. Contracts added from them: game-type presets, the `impact` caption style, the job form's options,
  `display_score`, custom templates (revision 0005), `GET /v1/options`.
- Split proportions: the owner said "about 50/50"; the screenshots measure 57% to 70% for the larger panel. Built
  to the screenshots, adjustable per template (`split_ratio`).
- #117 is treated as a money path: it changes no price, but `duration` and `limit` let a re-run that costs 1
  credit render up to 50 clips of up to 5 minutes. Left open with a recommendation.
- Bulk Edit (re-render), Bulk Schedule and Create Montage are shown as "Coming soon" until they exist; Bulk Export
  downloads the selected clips' existing files.
- `GET /v1/options` lists what the clip options accept, so the website shows a control only once the API accepts
  the option behind it. That lets the web work merge before or after #117 and B's PR.

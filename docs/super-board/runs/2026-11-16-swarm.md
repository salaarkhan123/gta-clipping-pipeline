## Decisions

- Parity is taken from Wayin's public docs only. Nothing was observed in their app. Undocumented behaviour is listed in PARITY.md under "Questions for the owner" and is not guessed.
- Our API keeps its own option names with Wayin's values and allowed sets, and today's defaults, until the owner answers PARITY.md questions 1 to 3.
- The orchestrator ships the shared schema (revision 0004) and wires `worker.py`, `main.py`, `config.py` and `models.py`; agents do not write migrations.
- Owner's answers to the seven questions (2026-11-16) are recorded in PARITY.md under "Decisions": our own API
  names with a mapping; Wayin's defaults (render, captions, reframe and hook off, 480p) with the dashboard
  sending explicit choices; `rank_index` and `timeline_index` both returned; Gameplay A/B provisional; enterprise
  switches not built; paid services priced before any integration; our own pricing, from measured costs.
- Merged by the owner: #112 (production-ready clips, `b28bc47`), #113 (contracts and revision 0004, `132b099`),
  #108 (SPEC section 14, `41f79b9`).

- Screenshots `w01` to `w12` arrived (2026-11-16). What they show is recorded in PARITY.md section 6 as observed,
  not verified. Contracts added from them: game-type presets, the `impact` caption style, the job form's options,
  `display_score`, custom templates (revision 0005), `GET /v1/options`.
- Split proportions: the owner said "about 50/50"; the screenshots measure 57% to 70% for the larger panel. Built
  to the screenshots, adjustable per template (`split_ratio`).
- #117 is treated as a money path: it changes no price, but `duration` and `limit` let a re-run that costs 1
  credit render up to 50 clips of up to 5 minutes. Left open with a recommendation.
- Bulk Edit (re-render), Bulk Schedule and Create Montage are shown as "Coming soon" until they exist; Bulk Export
  downloads the selected clips' existing files.
- `GET /v1/options` lists what the clip options accept, so the website shows a control only once the API accepts
  the option behind it. That lets the web work merge before or after #117 and B's PR.

- Owner's decisions, second set (2026-11-16): #117's charging rule approved (reserve the worst case, charge the
  seconds rendered, refund the rest; first run unchanged); split proportions follow the screenshots and are
  adjustable by the user; clip #4 ends 1:13:31; Coming-soon features stay visible and disabled and are still to be
  built; no feature is gated by plan yet; no paid Wayin exports, so output parity stays unverified; preset to
  screenshot mappings stay provisional.
- Owner's decisions on billing (2026-11-16): a refund or dispute removes the credits that payment granted and
  the balance stops at zero; the live prices on the server (Starter $19 / 600, Pro $39 / 1,500, Agency $149 /
  8,000) are confirmed.
- The spend limit stopped A2, C2 and D2 a second time and the session restarted, so they could not be resumed.
  Their partial work was saved as work-in-progress commits and fresh agents continue from it.
- A CI failure on #118 was the loudness test in `test_production_clips.py` (a peak of -0.8 dB against a limit of
  -1.0 dB after AAC encoding). It passed on the re-run and on every other PR. It is a flaky margin, not a
  regression; the limiter ceiling needs more headroom (render agent, later).

## Moment benchmark: Zias video against Wayin's top 15

Run on 2026-11-16 with the bucket branch (#117), `duration: 0-90`, no limit, **no AI judge** (no key on this
machine) and no rendering: selection only. Source: the 78-minute WebM the owner supplied.

- 22 moments in 234 s. Wayin shows 38.
- **7 of their 15 found** (10 s of overlap or more): #1 (our rank 1), #3, #5, #9, #11, #12, #15.
- **8 missed:**
  - #2, #13, #14: in the last two minutes and the first minute. The picker skips the start and end of a video
    (meant for "starting soon" screens); our first pick starts at 2:54 and our last ends at 1:14:19.
  - #4: directly before a moment we picked (1:13:33); the 60-second spacing rule keeps only one.
  - #6, #10: near misses. #6 overlaps our pick by just under 10 s; #10 ends 7 s before our pick starts.
  - #7 (22:18), #8 (33:22): nothing of ours within a minute.
- Our clips are 42 to 50 s long (the nominal 45 s: only the judge varies length); theirs are 36 to 92 s.
- Not measurable: our other 15 picks against their other 23 clips (not in the screenshot), titles, scores.
- Follow-ups for the engine, to be measured with the judge once a key is available: do not skip the edges in
  bucket mode; a shorter spacing rule for short buckets; more candidates per video.
